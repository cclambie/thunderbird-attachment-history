# Privacy Policy – Attachment History

Attachment History does not collect, transmit, or share any data. It makes no
network requests.

The extension stores a list of the attachments you save (file name, saved
location, date, and the subject and sender of the source email) in Thunderbird's
local extension storage on your computer, so it can show your history. You can
remove entries or clear the list at any time from the history page. Uninstalling
the extension deletes this list. Your saved files and emails are never modified
or deleted by the extension.
